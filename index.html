F L O A T I N G

Cards that float,
turn for everyone.

floating-board is a GTK4 widget that renders freely movable, rotatable and scalable cards on a transparent layer above your content — an in-app window manager for multi-touch tabletop surfaces where every side of the table is the front.

The widget

An in-app window manager,
made for tables.

On a table-top multitouch surface there is no "up" and no "down" — several users stand on different sides and interact with the same surface at once. FloatingBoard makes that practical.

Free card manipulation

Every card moves, rotates and scales independently through multi-touch gestures — drag, two-finger rotate, pinch, and an N-point transform tracker that combines all three at once.

Card chrome

Optional title header, fold-to-header, close button, resize and rotate grips, and selectable drag handles — headerless and fully chrome-less cards are supported too.

Virtual workspace & camera

Card geometry lives in workspace coordinates — FollowWidget, Fixed or Infinite. A View camera pans, zooms and rotates, with goto_card flights that center a card Prezi-style.

Tabletop seats

A Seat assigns cards to users on a table side — cards can be passed to the next player, landing in front of their seat in the right reading orientation.

Layout strategies

apply_layout arranges cards through smearor-commons-layout strategies — FlowLayout, SeatLayout or your own — animated via per-card frame-clock glides.

Boundary clamping

Cards keep a configurable minimum of their rotated footprint inside the bounds — live (Contained) or on gesture end (Loose). No card ever gets lost off-screen.

Serializable state

layout_snapshot() / restore_layout() produce a BoardLayout — geometry, fold state, seats, workspace and view — ready for TOML or JSON persistence.

Transparent overlay

Cards float above your content; gestures that miss every card fall through to the widgets underneath. No input grab, no modal layer — and independent touch sequences per card.

The stack

Built on Smearor
commons.

The widget ties together the shared, headless libraries of the Smearor ecosystem — geometry, animation and tabletop layouting — plus smearor-wrot-rotation for rotated GTK4 widgets.

Get started

Deal your first card.

Add the dependency, wrap your content in a FloatingBoard and put a card on the table.

Cargo.toml 
[dependencies]
floating-board = "0.1"
main.rs 
let board = FloatingBoard::new(&content_widget);
board.add_card(
    &controls_widget,
    CardConfig { title: Some("Controls".into()), ..Default::default() },
);

A full demo ships with the repository: cargo run --example demo.
