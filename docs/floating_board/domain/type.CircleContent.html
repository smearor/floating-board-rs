Variants§

§

Inscribed

The content is allocated the square inscribed in the inner shape (side = (d - 2·ring) / √2 on a Circle; the analogous inscribed square on polygons). By construction the content can never paint or catch picks outside the shape - the card keeps the full ring as its own surface.

§

Fill

The content is allocated the full inner region (side = d - 2·ring on a Circle; the rim-shrunk silhouette’s bounding box on polygons) and is expected to draw its own shape with transparent corners (a clock face, a pie menu, a chart).

§

Clipped

Like CardContent::Fill, plus a silhouette clip on the content: rendering stops at the inner shape, so the cut regions show the card - the ring stays a visible drag affordance even over a content that fills its square.

§

Cover

The content is allocated the whole bounding box - the card’s silhouette is the only bound, no ring stays card surface. The card’s own silhouette clip is the bound on every shape, so a content that paints its square still shows the silhouette. Ignored for Rectangle (its bounding box is the silhouette).
